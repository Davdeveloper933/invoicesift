Questions

How accurate is it?

Try it on your own invoices and see. Rather than guess, it leaves a field blank and flags the row when something can't be found or the numbers don't add up — so you know which rows to double-check.

Does it work with scanned or photographed invoices?

Yes. Pages without a text layer are read as images. Very blurry or handwritten invoices may be flagged for review.

What happens to my files?

Your PDFs are processed and deleted immediately — we don't keep them. The content is sent to our AI provider (OpenAI) for extraction, which does not use API data for training.

Which fields do you extract?

Supplier name, invoice number, invoice date, due date, subtotal, tax, total and currency. Need line items or other fields? Tell us — it helps us decide what to build next.

Can I connect it to my accounting software?

Today you get a CSV that opens in Excel or Google Sheets. If you'd want it sent straight to QuickBooks, Xero or another tool, tell us which one when you reserve your spot.
